A coach in central Texas wants soccer to be free — here's what we built to help make that happen
From: Erick Brambila To: Anthropic Social Impact Team Date: Draft — Oct 2026

Hey Anthropic team,

My name is Erick Brambila. I'm a DevOps engineer out of Liberty Hill, Texas. Most of the kids on our team are from Jarrell — small town north of Austin, working families, and youth sports can get expensive fast.

Our coach, Hugo, has this belief that's pretty simple: no kid should have to quit soccer because their family can't cover the fees. Registration, cleats, tournament travel — it adds up, and when it does, families just quietly disappear. No announcement, the kid just isn't at practice one week. Hugo wants to change that. His goal is to make the program free for any kid who needs it.

That stuck with me. So I built something.

I'm not a frontend developer. My background is DevOps and infrastructure. But over 12 sessions — about 62 hours — I built a full platform for the club using Claude Code:

  • 🌐  Bilingual website (EN + ES) — schedule with live weather, photo gallery, roster, news
  • ⚙️  Coach admin panel that auto-translates announcements so Hugo doesn't have to write everything twice
  • 📝  AI-generated match recaps in both languages
  • 🎥  KickReel — highlight pipeline where Hugo taps a button on the sideline camera, and by the time parents get home there's a 30-second clip of their kid on the site. Free. No subscription.

A boutique agency would quote $35,000–$50,000 for this. My total Claude API cost across those 62 hours was $215.

I bought the camera because I wanted something cool for the kids — that's on me. But the platform itself? $215.

You can see it live at legends-pitch.vercel.app

I'm not asking for money right now. I just wanted to put this in front of someone at Anthropic and see if it resonates.

This is what AI helping a real community looks like — not a demo, not a retrospective case study. A youth club in central Texas, a coach who genuinely believes soccer should be for every kid, and a parent who built the tools to help make that possible.

If there's someone on your team who works on social impact or community use cases, I'd love to talk.

Thanks for reading.

Erick Brambila Liberty Hill, TX
